How to build a timeless capsule wardrobe

Style Guide · 5 min read

A capsule wardrobe is a small, intentional collection of versatile pieces you love to wear. The idea is simple: fewer, better items that mix and match effortlessly.

Start with the essentials

Begin with neutral basics — a well-fitted tee, a classic shirt, and quality denim. These form the foundation that everything else builds on.

Add a few statement pieces

Once your basics are set, introduce a couple of bolder prints or colors to express your personality without overwhelming your collection.

"Elegance is refusal." — the art of choosing less, but better.

With a thoughtful capsule, getting dressed becomes effortless — and you always look put together.
